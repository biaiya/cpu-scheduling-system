First Come, First Served

The simplest CPU scheduling algorithm. The process that arrives first in the ready queue is the first to be executed, and it runs until it finishes. A long process at the front makes the shorter ones behind it wait.

Shortest Job First

A scheduling algorithm that picks the waiting process with the smallest burst time to run next. It gives a low average waiting time, but processes with long burst times may wait for a long time.

Priority

Each process is given a priority number, and the CPU goes to the process with the highest priority. In this system, a smaller number means a higher priority. Low priority processes may wait for a long time.

Round Robin

Each process is given a fixed amount of CPU time called a time quantum. When the quantum ends, the process goes to the back of the ready queue and the next process runs, until all processes are finished.
